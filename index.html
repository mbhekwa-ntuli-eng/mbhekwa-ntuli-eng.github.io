Two-Microphone Acoustic Direction-of-Arrival System

Developed a low-cost embedded acoustic sensing system using two microphones and an STM32 microcontroller to estimate the direction of a sound source. The project combined analogue signal conditioning, ADC/DMA sampling, Embedded C firmware, I²C communication, cross-correlation/TDOA signal processing, PCB development, oscilloscope validation, and systematic hardware–firmware debugging.
